# Story: Growth Tracking

**Epic:** [E6 - Advanced AI](../epic.md)
**Status:** not-started
**Priority:** P2
**Points:** 5
**Created:** 2024-12-08
**Updated:** 2024-12-08

## Objective
Help users see their personal growth over time with AI-generated quarterly reports that celebrate progress and gently note patterns.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Quarterly "growth report" comparing themes/sentiment over time
- [ ] Highlights positive changes and improvements
- [ ] Gently notes recurring challenges without judgment
- [ ] Encouraging, never judgmental tone
- [ ] Available on-demand in Insights page

## Implementation Notes
- Requires sufficient entry history (suggest 50+ entries)
- Compare current quarter themes to previous quarters
- Identify trends: improving, stable, or concerning
- Generate narrative summary with specific examples
- Consider storing reports for historical comparison

## Test Plan
- Test with users having 3+ months of entries
- Verify positive framing of all feedback
- Test empty state for new users
- Verify report regeneration works
